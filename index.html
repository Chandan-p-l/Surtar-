The Reed Mechanics

Harmonium bellows control air pressure. Play with **Manual Bellows** for complete expression:

  • Tap Spacebar repeatedly to pump air.
  • No wind pressure = No Sound!
  • Toggle **Bass, Male, Female** stops to dynamically mix harmonium reeds.

Grand Piano Mode

Switch to Grand Piano for resonant physical modeling waveforms.

  • Press & hold Spacebar for the Sustain Pedal.
  • Sustain holds notes running even after releasing QWERTY keys.
  • Adjust the **Sound Decay** slider for room reverberation depth.
